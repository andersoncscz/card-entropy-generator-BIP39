Where does the entropy come from?

The randomness comes from you physically shuffling a real deck of cards — not from this computer. Shuffle a standard 52-card deck by hand, then click each card here in the exact order it appears in your shuffled deck. This tool only records that order and converts it to entropy; it never generates randomness itself.

How much entropy do I get?

The number of distinct orderings of a 52-card deck is 52! (52 factorial) — approximately 8.0658×1067, or about 225 bits of entropy when you enter the full deck. Entering fewer cards gives proportionally less entropy, which is why entering the whole deck is recommended.

How do I reach 256 bits (24 words)?

One deck tops out at ~225 bits, so for a full 256-bit / 24-word seed you need a second, independent shuffle. Enter your deck, then physically reshuffle it and press “Reshuffle & Continue” to start a new round — its entropy adds on top. About 6 more cards from the reshuffled deck is enough. The deck locks automatically once you reach 256 bits.

A well-shuffled physical deck is a strong, computer-independent source of randomness. For real funds, do this offline and make sure your shuffle is thorough.
